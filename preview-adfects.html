Campaign description
Headliner Music Academy, a music education school in Sacramento, CA, sends SMS messages to enrolled students and their parents or guardians. Messages serve two purposes: (1) Customer care — class reminders, schedule changes, instructor rescheduling, and billing updates; and (2) Marketing — promotional announcements about upcoming programs, open enrollment, and academy events. Recipients opt in by submitting their phone number through the signup form at headlinermusicacademy.com and checking an unchecked consent checkbox. Opt-in is optional and not required to enroll. All messages include opt-out instructions (reply STOP). Message frequency varies.
How users consent
End users opt in to receive SMS messages from Headliner Music Academy by submitting their mobile phone number through the signup form at headlinermusicacademy.com. The form includes an unchecked checkbox that states: "By checking this box, I agree to receive SMS messages from Headliner Music Academy regarding scheduling, billing, reminders, and account updates. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out at any time." Providing a phone number and opting in is optional and not required to enroll or receive services.
