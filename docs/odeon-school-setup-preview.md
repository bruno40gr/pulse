# School Setup — owner-facing prototype

Local entry: http://127.0.0.1:3197/school-setup (also linked from /odeon).
Repository route: /content-studio-prototype/school-setup, development-only and owner/admin gated.

## Audience and IA
Academy owner or administrator configuring the school. No walkthrough, explored-progress mechanic, or internal architectural explanations in the interface.

The default entry is a new account with no saved records. A New account / Configured school switch exposes both experiences, with separate in-memory state. Direct links use `?experience=new` or `?experience=configured`; section navigation preserves the experience. The new-account overview shows setup progress and Start/Continue setup. Empty sections open directly into their first form; Edit details is reserved for existing singleton records. The configured experience summarizes fictional records.

Six sections: School details; Locations & rooms; Services & programs; Plans & prices; Teachers; Calendar & policies. Add/Edit opens a full-page form with Save changes and Cancel in the header. Edits are drafted separately and validated before updating preview state. School details and policies are singleton records. Plan/teacher forms validate their referenced services and locations. No eyebrow labels or preview/reset notices appear in the UI. Selected navigation and experience controls use espresso on cream, and creation/save actions use the shared primary button. The standalone local root loads bundled DM Sans through next/font/local.

## Direct setup and record navigation
- Empty sections open their first form immediately, without an empty-state or Set up button gate.
- Unsaved-change confirmation compares values and rooms with the initial draft; untouched forms and reverted edits do not prompt. Cancel and editor-back share the guard.
- First-level section screens have no overview back arrow. Nested editors show back-arrow navigation and named headings, such as Edit “Headliner Rocklin” and Edit Piano.
- School details include optional EIN/tax number, sales tax ID and a local PNG/JPEG/WebP logo preview (5 MB limit). No tax verification or upload service is connected.
- Locations use text address fields and repeatable rooms, each with Room name, positive capacity and a services multiselect defaulting to All, including services created later. Specific services can be selected instead. These three controls share one desktop row and stack on narrow screens. Add/remove room actions update the draft. Services can be assigned after they have been created; no room dropdown or comma-separated room field remains.

A persistent progress bar shows the number and percentage of sections with saved records across the overview and section screens. Unsaved drafts do not count as completed.

## Working preview behavior
- Add/edit locations, services, plans and teachers. Edit school details and policies.
- New services and locations appear in plan/teacher selectors by stable record ID.
- Multiple pricing bases and cadences coexist. Packs/prepaid terms require once-only billing and a positive visit count.
- Required values, money precision, discounts, durations, private capacity, working hours/days and closure ordering are checked.
- Navigation keeps state within School Setup; reload resets it. No API, storage or live configuration writes.

## Deliberately limited
This is an IA/interaction prototype, not a completed academy configuration engine. Working days remain comma-separated inputs; one teaching assignment and regular hours block per teacher; one closure in school policies. No record archive/delete, multiple teacher capability rows, exceptions, server-backed branding upload, detailed discount eligibility, effective dates, policy versioning, persistence or publication. Those remain future work. Do not use the fixture prices/policies as authoritative school values.

The catalog research at /Users/brunowong/pulse/docs/service-catalogue-model.md remains the model direction; no catalog migration is implemented. Setup is not connected to enrollment, the staff workspace or simulated invoice generation. Existing full-month/no-automatic-proration invoice behavior remains untouched. Real payments remain the final milestone.

## Curriculum section mockup

Plans & prices defines school-wide, reusable pricing models independently of services and appears before Services & programs in navigation and setup order. The reusable-model helper text lives only on the main Plans & prices page, not in the editor. Models can be created before the service catalogue exists and have no service field or required service reference. Each service selects zero or more shared models in Edit Service → Pricing models; the same model can be selected by multiple services. Service assignments commit/cancel with the service draft and new services start with no pricing assignments. Prices and billing rules are managed centrally in Plans & prices.

There is no editable plan name. Labels are generated from purchase option, price, cadence/basis or included lessons, plus the applicable first-period rule and any nonzero fees/deposits. Subscriptions offer Monthly, Weekly or Every two weeks, never Once. Per-lesson subscriptions offer Remaining scheduled lessons or Full amount for the first period; fixed subscriptions offer Full amount or calendar/scheduled-lesson proration. Single lessons, lesson packs and prepaid terms use a fixed one-time payment, hide cadence/basis/first-period controls, and discard first-period values. Only packs/terms show Included lessons. Returning to Subscription restores a valid recurring cadence and first-period option. Shared `pricingFields`, `normalizePricing` and `pricingName` functions drive form visibility/options, validation, saved labels and normalized state.

School-wide discounts live in a separate Discounts panel on the main Plans & prices page. Each rule has a free-entry name, positive percentage (up to 100%) or fixed USD amount, and one or more structured eligibility conditions. Each condition stores a field key, comparison and typed value, never an arbitrary text instruction. The extensible `discountFields` catalogue currently includes a fixed family/sibling condition, student active-enrollment count, membership duration in completed months, age in completed years, enrollment lesson length, progression level number, enrollment frequency and enrollment status. The family condition means another actively enrolled student in the same family (two or more in total), with no comparison or number to configure; its eligibility check uses Yes/No/Not provided. Internally it keeps a fixed >= 2 threshold and rejects alternative thresholds. Other numeric fields support equals/not-equal/at-least/more-than/at-most/less-than; choice fields allow equals/not-equal and valid enum values only. Field changes reset the comparison/value to a valid combination. Each field explains the data meaning and units. Staff/custom-text presets have been removed because no reliable staff eligibility source or custom-text interpreter is connected.

Rules support All or Any matching. Check eligibility accepts case values without saving them and calls the same pure `evaluateDiscount` function used in regression tests. Missing/invalid data yields an indeterminate result unless another condition already decides an All/Any outcome. The rule evaluator accepts typed facts; fetching facts from enrollment/account records and applying a discount to invoices are not connected by this change. Rules can be added, edited, cancelled or deleted with confirmation. Each draft owns copied conditions; new/configured experiences remain isolated. Unsaved changes participate in navigation confirmation. Discounts are not stored in pricing models.

The first visit to Plans & prices shows the main page; Add plan explicitly opens the editor. Empty pricing models, discounts, new-account overview tiles and empty room lists use the shared `EmptyState` component, matching the existing shared Curriculum and service-material empty states. Pricing uses the panel variant with a wallet icon, not a plain-text placeholder.

Curriculum is a sidebar destination (`section=curriculum`), supplementary to the six required setup-progress sections. It contains school-wide level names and the reusable Books & materials library. Level names are no longer edited or displayed in School details; Curriculum is their single UI home. Names remain shared across services and teacher eligibility, while stable IDs preserve assignments. New-account and configured-school state remain isolated, including names and library contents.

The library is the material source, with helper text instead of a search field, adding/editing a Book name and USD cost, and an optional local PDF under 10 MB. Save commits the library draft; Cancel drops it. Navigation prompts for changed Curriculum drafts. Service assignment save/cancel remains independent. Renaming levels or editing library records does not copy assignments into new services. Learning goals/content per service and level is a future extension, not part of this mockup.

Service level cards include a muted shared FieldLabel, Books & materials, with a 26px indentation matching the checkbox-to-label inset. Service name is H2 like the other peer sections and the previous horizontal divider is removed. Browser regression covers Curriculum search, level rename propagation, PDF library creation, service assignment isolation, and leaving changed Curriculum drafts.

### Progression configuration
The section is titled Set up progression levels. The shared table presents five vertical rows, Level 1–5 plus a name column; editing provides an enable checkbox and name input per row. Levels 1–3 are enabled initially. Disabled rows retain their names and assignment IDs but are unavailable in service/teacher selectors until enabled. At least one enabled level and a name for each enabled level are required. Saved rows remain vertical in the same order. Cancel replaces Cancel level names.

Books & materials has explanatory helper text and no library search. The copy describes required materials and costs in the parent portal and automatic invoicing when a student starts a progression level. This change adds that product copy, not billing integration. No prototype disclaimers appear in the UI. Each library row has 16px vertical padding and a divider. Edit expands beneath the selected record with local header actions; Add opens above the retained list. Opening, saving and cancelling preserve page position without autofocus. The field label is Book name. Browser regression checks later-row editing, opening/save/cancel scroll offsets on desktop/mobile, five editable level rows, enabling Level 4 and name propagation, and the disabled Level 5 input.

### Service images
Every service editor includes Service image with a PNG/JPEG/WebP upload under 5 MB, preview and removal. Files are decoded before acceptance and invalid/unreadable files show an inline error. Image changes follow the service draft save/cancel boundary and are not inherited by new services. A bundled seven-photo Pexels library suggests images using service-name keywords (piano, guitar, voice, drums and strings); unknown names show the full library, and Browse all images is always available when suggestions are shown. Matching is local keyword matching, not a live search or AI API. Photos retain their Pexels source URL and the UI links to the Pexels license. Local assets are under `public/images/services`; IDs and source URLs live in `service-images.ts`. Pexels license checked November 9, 2026. Browser regression covers relevant suggestions, full-library browsing, valid/invalid upload, saved selection and cancelled removal.

### Lightweight discount policy
Plans & prices presents pricing model cards first, then Discount policy, then Discounts. These are direct children of the same shared records stack with a consistent 18px gap. Discount policy defaults to Best single discount; Combine eligible discounts progressively reveals Percentage discounts first or Fixed-amount discounts first. Copy specifies sequential application to the remaining balance and a zero floor. Save/Cancel and account-experience isolation are preserved; editing policy and discount drafts is mutually exclusive. This is configuration UI, not a new invoice calculation engine.

Discounts have optional Starts on / Ends on dates, with blank dates meaning no time limit, inclusive end-date copy in the school timezone, and valid-date/range validation. Dates are saved and displayed with each rule. No redemption limits, caps, custom-priority editor or separate benefit-duration machinery were added. Existing eligibility checks remain condition-only; dates and combination policies are not wired into invoice generation. Browser coverage checks section order, policy choices and saved dates on desktop/mobile.
